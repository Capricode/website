Swiss-O-Week 2021

Official companion app for the multi-day orienteering event Swiss-O-Week 2021 in Arosa.

Push notifications, plus everything about the event and each stage. Follow your friends, see their start times for every stage day and check results as they come in.

Platforms
iOS, Android
Built with
Xamarin.Forms, ReactiveUI
Languages
German, English

MyOApp

Every orienteering event in Switzerland, in one place. See what’s on near you.

Pick your regions and favorite events, and they show up on your dashboard. Open an event for directions, start lists, results, maps and more. Comes in light and dark.

Platforms
iOS, Android
Built with
Xamarin.Forms
Languages
Swiss German, German, French, English, Polish
